## Sweet and Light: When You Want Butterflies

Some evenings call for warmth. You want a story where the leads make each other laugh, where the tension comes from will-they-won-they rather than life-or-death stakes. These are the dramas that leave you smiling, the ones where love feels almost easy -- and that is a good thing. If you are new to the genre or just need something gentle after a rough week, start here. You can also [browse all romantic dramas](/en/best/romantic) in our full collection or [explore more light and fun picks](/en/best/light-fun).

### Falling Into Your Smile (2021)
![Falling Into Your Smile](https://image.tmdb.org/t/p/w342/vvkzt9lTgTaznMHNIpqHNge8cl6.jpg)

In the competitive world of professional e-sports, a determined rookie player joins an all-male team and sparks a slow-burn connection with the cold but brilliant team captain. If you have ever watched a rom-com and wished it had more banter, sharper chemistry, and a setting you have never seen before, this is your next watch. The romance grows naturally through teamwork, teasing, and those quiet moments when he starts noticing her in ways he cannot explain. We recommend this for viewers who enjoy modern-day settings, competitive environments, and a heroine who holds her own. At 31 episodes, it moves at a comfortable pace. [Falling Into Your Smile](/en/drama/falling-into-your-smile) remains one of the most rewatchable sweet romances in recent years.

### Are You The One (2024)
![Are You The One](https://image.tmdb.org/t/p/w342/xpfghVGStd3tVzh7YSOm9WcckZ3.jpg)

A brave female bandit leader and a clever, underestimated prince cross paths during a mission and reluctantly team up for justice -- and unexpected love. This drama wraps its romance inside a light adventure framework, which means you get action, clever plotting, and genuine comedic timing alongside the growing affection between the leads. The sweet mood here comes from how they challenge each other: she is fierce and independent, he is strategic and patient, and their dynamic feels earned rather than forced. If you enjoy period settings with a playful energy and a couple who act as genuine partners, this 40-episode series is an excellent pick. [Are You The One](/en/drama/are-you-the-one) is ideal for viewers who want something historical but not heavy, romantic but never boring.

### The Legends (2019)
![The Legends](https://image.tmdb.org/t/p/w342/14lWfiTokkzHR9HY46n6MyqSXEd.jpg)

A demon path seeker with a mysterious past and a righteous immortal from a prestigious sect clash over ideology -- and then slowly fall for each other across a world of supernatural creatures and ancient rivalries. The sweetness here is layered with fantasy worldbuilding, which gives the romance room to breathe beyond simple attraction. Their relationship develops through shared danger, reluctant trust, and the kind of quiet devotion that makes you clutch your pillow. At 56 episodes, there is plenty of time for the leads to grow into something real. We recommend [The Legends](/en/drama/the-legends) for fans of xianxia who want the romance to feel central rather than secondary. If you enjoy mythological settings with strong emotional stakes and a lead couple who fight side by side, this one delivers.

### Joy of Life (2019)
![Joy of Life](https://image.tmdb.org/t/p/w342/wHJvPo9CLpXwX1ncDg6uD0QJIZo.jpg)

A young man with mysterious abilities and a sharp mind navigates deadly court intrigue using humor and wit, all while an understated love story quietly develops alongside him. This is a slightly unusual pick for a sweet mood list because the romance is not the main plot -- but that is precisely what makes it work. The warmth comes from the protagonist's unshakable optimism and the dry, affectionate way he relates to the people closest to him. At 46 episodes set in a richly detailed ancient world, it balances political tension with genuine levity. If you want a drama where the romance is a slow simmer rather than the main dish, and where the lead makes you laugh out loud, [Joy of Life](/en/drama/joy-of-life) is a standout choice. Perfect for viewers who appreciate clever writing and want something with more plot depth than a typical romance.

## Heartbreaking: When You Want to Feel Everything

There is a reason people reach for tissues after certain dramas. The stories in this section are not sad for the sake of sadness -- they hurt because the love feels real, because the obstacles are genuine, and because sometimes the most beautiful romances are the ones that break you open. If you have been avoiding emotions all day and now want to release them, these are the ones. Head over to [browse all tearjerker dramas](/en/best/wanna-cry) for more picks in this category.

### With You (2016)
![With You](https://image.tmdb.org/t/p/w342/jBiWSSukY3hjy7iYC9bMmZ2JXvr.jpg)

High school deskmates Geng Geng and Yu Huai slowly fall in love through shared classes, quiet glances, and the painful pressure of growing up in a world that does not make room for young love. This is the drama that redefined what a campus romance could be in Chinese television. It fits the heartbreaking mood because nothing is melodramatic -- the pain comes from how accurately it captures the helplessness of first love, family expectations, and the gap between desire and reality. At just 24 episodes, every scene counts. If you have ever had a desk-mate you cared about more than you admitted, [With You](/en/drama/with-you) will find the part of you that still remembers. We recommend it for anyone who wants a romance that feels less like fiction and more like memory.

### Never Gone (2016)
![Never Gone](https://image.tmdb.org/t/p/w342/aELjDBn3eQPxq7AvCCiznAvdjUo.jpg)

A devoted young man and a complicated, guarded woman navigate love across years of separation, misunderstanding, and the quiet damage that time does to the people who care most. This is the gold standard of the angsty chinese romance drama. Every misunderstanding feels human. Every reunion carries the weight of everything unsaid. At 14 episodes, it is short, intense, and devastating. If you want a story about love that persists despite everything, [Never Gone](/en/drama/never-gone) is essential viewing. Recommended for those who prefer quality over quantity and are not afraid of a story that refuses to offer easy comfort.

### My Huckleberry Friends (2017)
![My Huckleberry Friends](https://image.tmdb.org/t/p/w342/pxTwQRcpa32fhaBALgzUxqVr2sM.jpg)

A bright, determined girl and a cheerful, easygoing boy navigate high school love through intertwined timelines that jump between their teenage years and adult lives. The structure itself is what makes this drama so emotionally powerful -- you already know where they end up, and that knowledge turns every sweet moment into something bittersweet. It fits the heartbreaking category because nostalgia is its own kind of pain. At 30 episodes, it takes its time building the small, ordinary moments that become unforgettable in retrospect. [My Huckleberry Friends](/en/drama/my-huckleberry-friends) is for viewers who love stories about growing up and the love stories we outgrow.

### Ashes of Love (2018)
![Ashes of Love](https://image.tmdb.org/t/p/w342/oQOJWltp5UYf76EO1KJCkXjdjIJ.jpg)

A flower deity raised without the ability to feel love crosses paths with a crown prince and slowly discovers what it means to care for someone -- right as the world conspires to tear them apart. This xianxia epic is a landmark of the genre, and the heartbreaking mood here comes from a cruel irony: the moment she finally understands love, everything threatens to destroy it. Over 63 episodes, the story builds a sprawling supernatural world where personal feelings collide with cosmic duty. If you enjoy fantasy settings with epic stakes and a romance fought against destiny itself, [Ashes of Love](/en/drama/ashes-of-love) is the definitive choice. Recommended for viewers who want a long, immersive journey with a love story that earns every emotional beat.

## Intense and Complex: When Love Is Never Simple

Not every romance is gentle. Some stories place love in the middle of power struggles, betrayal, and impossible moral choices. The relationships in this section are intense not because the characters shout at each other, but because the stakes are impossibly high and every decision has consequences. These dramas ask: what would you sacrifice for the person you love, and what would you sacrifice to survive? For more dramas with depth and tension, you can [browse all romantic dramas](/en/best/romantic) in our collection.

### The Double (2024)
![The Double](https://image.tmdb.org/t/p/w342/pag1eUvkMOUgT6UCOCgLr1Ibo8U.jpg)

A magistrate's daughter survives a brutal assassination attempt orchestrated by her own husband and returns with a new identity, plotting revenge alongside a mysterious ally whose motives remain unclear. The romance here is built on mutual suspicion, shared secrets, and the kind of tension that comes from never knowing who you can trust. It fits the intense mood because love develops in a space where survival always comes first. At 40 episodes, the pacing is relentless. If you want a drama where the leads are genuinely dangerous to each other and yet impossible to look away from, [The Double](/en/drama/the-double) is a masterclass. Recommended for viewers who enjoy revenge plots, morally ambiguous characters, and a romance that simmers beneath layers of calculation.

### Story of Minglan (2018)
![Story of Minglan](https://image.tmdb.org/t/p/w342/5R1YSPffQWEOwlXVAwxPYUmRH4z.jpg)

An unloved concubine-born daughter in the Song Dynasty navigates court politics, family schemes, and societal constraints while finding love with a powerful man who sees her worth when no one else does. This is not a simple romance -- it is a meticulously crafted portrait of a woman who uses intelligence and patience to survive a world designed to keep her powerless. The love story is all the more powerful because it arrives as partnership, not rescue. At 78 episodes, it is the longest on this list, and every episode earns its place. [Story of Minglan](/en/drama/story-of-minglan) is for viewers who want a period drama with extraordinary depth, where the romance is woven into a larger tapestry of ambition, family, and survival.

### The Prisoner of Beauty (2025)
![The Prisoner of Beauty](https://image.tmdb.org/t/p/w342/AZdlvg8Rij2bBkV0V9vJxsejZ1.jpg)

A brilliant woman from a war-torn era becomes entangled with a powerful warlord whose strength and ambition make him both protector and threat. The intense mood here comes from the power imbalance at the heart of the relationship -- she is brilliant and defiant, he is commanding and possessive, and their attraction is charged with the knowledge that trust could mean vulnerability in a world that punishes both. At 36 episodes, the drama balances political intrigue with emotional depth. [The Prisoner of Beauty](/en/drama/the-prisoner-of-beauty) is recommended for viewers who enjoy historical settings with dark undertones, complex power dynamics, and a romance that is as much about negotiation as it is about feeling.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the best Chinese romance drama for beginners?

If you are new to Chinese dramas and want something accessible, start with a sweet and light pick like [Falling Into Your Smile](/en/drama/falling-into-your-smile). Modern settings, relatable characters, and a straightforward romance make it an easy entry point. You can also check our [starter pack](/en/starter-pack) for a curated list of beginner-friendly dramas across genres.

### Are Chinese romance dramas always set in ancient times?

Not at all. Chinese romance dramas span modern and historical settings in roughly equal measure. Modern romances like [Falling Into Your Smile](/en/drama/falling-into-your-smile) and [With You](/en/drama/with-you) are set in contemporary schools, offices, and cities. Historical romances like [Story of Minglan](/en/drama/story-of-minglan) and [Ashes of Love](/en/drama/ashes-of-love) offer richly detailed period worlds. Pick whichever setting appeals to you -- both have exceptional options.

### How long are most Chinese romance dramas?

Episode counts vary widely. Short-form dramas like [Never Gone](/en/drama/never-gone) run around 14 episodes, while epic romances like [Story of Minglan](/en/drama/story-of-minglan) can stretch to 78 episodes. Most fall in the 24 to 46 episode range. If you prefer shorter commitments, look for dramas under 30 episodes. If you want something to settle into over a longer period, 40-plus episodes gives the romance room to develop gradually.

### Where can I watch these dramas with English subtitles?

Most of the dramas listed here are available on major streaming platforms such as Viki, YouTube (official channels), iQIYI, and Tencent Video, all with English subtitles.
