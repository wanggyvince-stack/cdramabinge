# 10 Best Chinese Thriller & Mystery Dramas to Binge Right Now

Chinese thriller dramas and Chinese mystery dramas have quietly become some of the most compelling offerings in global television. Whether you are drawn to tightly written crime procedurals, layered political intrigue, or science fiction that bends your sense of reality, this list has something for every suspense lover. Over the past decade, mainland Chinese production studios have invested heavily in short-form, high-concept storytelling that rivals anything on Western streaming platforms. The result is a growing catalog of [thriller dramas](/en/best/thriller) and [mystery dramas](/en/best/mystery) that deliver tight plotting, morally complex characters, and endings that stay with you long after the credits roll. Below, we have ranked ten of the best Chinese thriller and mystery series available to stream right now, ordered by audience rating.

---

## 1. Moral Peanuts Finale (2015)
![Moral Peanuts Finale](https://image.tmdb.org/t/p/w342/mRHRrlH7LXezAbdxuqWaKvBghqY.jpg)

**Rating:** 9.7/10 | **Episodes:** 10 | **Genres:** [thriller dramas](/en/best/thriller), crime

When Zhao Ning's father is driven to suicide by an elaborate fraud ring, she refuses to let the perpetrators walk away. Instead, she quietly assembles a team of specialists — forgers, hackers, con artists — and sets out to dismantle the criminal network piece by piece. [Moral Peanuts Finale](/en/drama/moral-peanuts-finale) is a masterclass in escalation, with each episode revealing a new layer of deception that recontextualizes everything before it. The writing is razor-sharp, the pacing relentless, and the moral ambiguity keeps you questioning who the real villains are. **Mood:** Mindbending, intense.

## 2. The Long Season (2023)
![The Long Season](https://image.tmdb.org/t/p/w342/tJLiKDYdfMskFkJXV1HnaQAdpGf.jpg)

**Rating:** 9.4/10 | **Episodes:** 12 | **Genres:** drama, crime, [thriller dramas](/en/best/thriller)

In the autumn of 1997, a dismembered corpse is pulled from a river in a small northeastern industrial town. A retired taxi driver with a sharp eye for detail begins cooperating with local police to piece together the victim's identity. [The Long Season](/en/drama/the-long-season) is less a conventional whodunit than a meditation on how a single unsolved crime fractures an entire community across decades. The direction is atmospheric and unhurried, the performances grounded, and the finale earns every bit of its emotional weight. **Mood:** Intense, mindbending.

## 3. Nirvana in Fire (2015)
![Nirvana in Fire](https://image.tmdb.org/t/p/w342/pzDC9rdyJhGdgVGoYuAj8LTHBtJ.jpg)

**Rating:** 9.1/10 | **Episodes:** 54 | **Genres:** historical, drama, [mystery dramas](/en/best/mystery)

Twelve years after his entire army is massacred under false charges, the sole survivor resurfaces in the capital under a new identity — a frail, unassuming strategist who slowly infiltrates the imperial court. [Nirvana in Fire](/en/drama/nirvana-in-fire) is a slow-burn political mystery wrapped in historical costume drama. What sets it apart is its patience: every alliance, betrayal, and reversal is planted episodes in advance. It rewards viewers willing to invest in its longer arc, and the payoffs are extraordinary. **Mood:** Mindbending, empowering.

## 4. The Long Night (2020)
![The Long Night](https://image.tmdb.org/t/p/w342/wU5L7mZA0Yy764D1WPP2403d0MG.jpg)

**Rating:** 9.0/10 | **Episodes:** 12 | **Genres:** [thriller dramas](/en/best/thriller), crime, mystery

A prosecutor stumbles upon evidence suggesting that a teacher's death — officially ruled an accident — may have been covered up by officials with significant political leverage. [The Long Night](/en/drama/the-long-night) is structured as three interlocking timelines, each revealing a different facet of the same conspiracy. The series does not flinch from depicting how institutional power can silence individuals, and its emotional core hits harder than any action sequence could. Bring tissues. **Mood:** Intense, wanna_cry.

## 5. Day and Night (2017)
![Day and Night](https://image.tmdb.org/t/p/w342/cMUymzvJL0MeXR7OuuBmR0KGaxZ.jpg)

**Rating:** 8.9/10 | **Episodes:** 32 | **Genres:** [thriller dramas](/en/best/thriller), crime

Twin brothers separated in childhood grow up on opposite sides of the law — one becomes a feared criminal figure, the other a dedicated police officer. When their paths collide, neither man can fully trust what the other represents. [Day and Night](/en/drama/day-and-night) thrives on its central duality, using the twin premise not as a gimmick but as a genuine exploration of nature versus nurture. The crime plot is layered and unpredictable, and the brother-versus-brother tension sustains all 32 episodes. **Mood:** Intense, mindbending.

## 6. The Bad Kids (2020)
![The Bad Kids](https://image.tmdb.org/t/p/w342/zlaml7IKAIPa3NJE9LhRveELD0v.jpg)

**Rating:** 8.8/10 | **Episodes:** 12 | **Genres:** [thriller dramas](/en/best/thriller), crime, mystery

Three children on a summer outing accidentally capture a murder on camera. Their decision about what to do with the footage sets off a chain reaction that entangles multiple families in lies and cover-ups. [The Bad Kids](/en/drama/the-bad-kids) is a tightly wound 12-episode thriller that examines how ordinary people rationalize increasingly dangerous choices. The child performances are remarkably natural, and the script never talks down to its audience. It is the kind of series you finish in one sitting. **Mood:** Intense, spooky.

## 7. Three-Body (2023)
![Three-Body](https://image.tmdb.org/t/p/w342/q2sNliRi4j0ncXKUO1x0MldR20A.jpg)

**Rating:** 8.8/10 | **Episodes:** 30 | **Genres:** sci-fi, [mystery dramas](/en/best/mystery)

After a string of physicists take their own lives, a nanoscientist is drawn into a mysterious virtual reality game that may hold the key to understanding why. [Three-Body](/en/drama/three-body) adapts Liu Cixin's celebrated novel with remarkable fidelity, translating its dense scientific concepts into visual storytelling without dumbing anything down. The production design is ambitious, and the sense of cosmic dread builds slowly and convincingly across its 30-episode run. **Mood:** Mindbending, intense.

## 8. The Knockout (2023)
![The Knockout](https://image.tmdb.org/t/p/w342/6F2UcY1p2YCz3xgLz6NfDh81QC3.jpg)

**Rating:** 8.7/10 | **Episodes:** 39 | **Genres:** crime, drama, [thriller dramas](/en/best/thriller)

Spanning two timelines across twenty years, this series follows a principled police officer and a humble fish vendor whose lives become inextricably linked — one ascending through the ranks of law enforcement, the other transforming into a powerful crime lord. [The Knockout](/en/drama/the-knockout) is a sprawling crime saga that examines the slow corrosion of morality under economic pressure. Its dual-timeline structure gives the story an unusual depth, allowing viewers to witness both the rise and the reckoning. **Mood:** Intense.

## 9. Under the Skin (2022)
![Under the Skin](https://image.tmdb.org/t/p/w342/k2n41i7V8OQiGXWKtZ8iTPlSiQH.jpg)

**Rating:** 8.7/10 | **Episodes:** 20 | **Genres:** [mystery dramas](/en/best/mystery), crime

A portrait artist's casual sketch inadvertently leads to the death of an undercover detective five years earlier. Now haunted by guilt, he is pulled back into the criminal underworld when a new case forces him to confront what his art set in motion. [Under the Skin](/en/drama/under-the-skin) is an unconventional mystery that uses visual art as both clue and catalyst. The procedural elements are solid, but it is the psychological weight of the protagonist's guilt that gives the series its distinctive gravity. **Mood:** Mindbending.

## 10. Reset (2022)
![Reset](https://image.tmdb.org/t/p/w342/3vbovxhpmrHbzOt9AhzLziaxTO.jpg)

**Rating:** 8.5/10 | **Episodes:** 15 | **Genres:** [thriller dramas](/en/best/thriller), mystery, sci-fi

A college student and a game designer wake up on a bus moments before it explodes — and then wake up again, and again, trapped in a repeating loop that they must break to survive. [Reset](/en/drama/reset) is a lean, efficient 15-episode series that takes a familiar time-loop concept and executes it with precision. Each iteration reveals new information, and the chemistry between the two leads carries the emotional stakes. It is one of the most accessible entry points for viewers new to Chinese dramas. **Mood:** Intense, mindbending.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the highest-rated Chinese thriller drama?

Moral Peanuts Finale holds the top spot with a 9.7/10 rating. It is a 10-episode crime thriller about a team of con artists targeting a fraud ring, praised for its tight writing and unpredictable twists.

### Are Chinese mystery dramas available with English subtitles?

Yes. All ten series listed above are available on major international streaming platforms with English subtitles. Some, like Three-Body, have official Netflix releases with professionally translated subtitles.

### Which Chinese thriller drama is best for beginners?

Reset is widely recommended as an accessible starting point. It runs just 15 episodes, uses a familiar time-loop premise, and maintains a brisk pace throughout. The Bad Kids is another strong choice at 12 episodes.

### What makes Chinese thrillers different from Western ones?

Chinese thriller dramas often place greater emphasis on social context and institutional dynamics. Rather than focusing solely on the individual detective or criminal, these series frequently explore how broader social pressures, economic inequality, and bureaucratic structures shape the events on screen. The pacing also tends to be more deliberate, prioritizing character development and atmosphere over constant action.
