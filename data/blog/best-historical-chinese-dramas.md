# The 12 Best Chinese Period Dramas, Ranked by Rating

Historical Chinese dramas have captivated viewers worldwide, and for good reason. From the treacherous corridors of imperial courts to misty mountain landscapes where immortal warriors clash, Chinese period dramas offer a storytelling depth and visual grandeur that few other genres can match. Whether you are drawn to intricate palace scheming, [wuxia](/en/best/wuxia) swordsmanship, or [fantasy](/en/best/fantasy) romance set against breathtaking ancient backdrops, this list has something for every viewer.

We have ranked 12 of the best historical Chinese dramas by audience rating, grouped by genre so you can find exactly what suits your mood. Each entry includes a one-line synopsis, full episode count, and why it deserves a spot on your watchlist. If you cannot get enough of these settings, also check out our curated [historical drama recommendations](/en/best/historical) page for even more titles across every sub-genre.

**Court Intrigue: Power, Politics, and Betrayal**

## Nirvana in Fire (2015) — Strategic Revenge
![Nirvana in Fire](https://image.tmdb.org/t/p/w342/pzDC9rdyJhGdgVGoYuAj8LTHBtJ.jpg)

**Rating:** 9.1 | **Episodes:** 54 | **Genres:** historical, drama, mystery

A sole survivor of a massacred army resurfaces years later as a frail but brilliant strategist, weaving an elaborate web of revenge inside the imperial court. [Nirvana in Fire](/en/drama/nirvana-in-fire) is widely regarded as the gold standard of Chinese political drama. What sets it apart is the chess-like precision of its plotting: every conversation is a calculated move, every alliance a potential trap, and the protagonist's slow dismantling of his enemies is deeply satisfying. The production design is meticulous, the supporting cast is outstanding, and the 54-episode runtime never drags. If you want court intrigue done right, this is where you start.

## Story of Minglan (2018) — Northern Song Dynasty Romance
![Story of Minglan](https://image.tmdb.org/t/p/w342/5R1YSPffQWEOwlXVAwxPYUmRH4z.jpg)

**Rating:** 8.8 | **Episodes:** 78 | **Genres:** historical, romance, drama

An unloved concubine-born daughter quietly navigates the complex politics of a Northern Song aristocratic household, finding love and asserting her independence along the way. [Story of Minglan](/en/drama/story-of-minglan) is a masterclass in patience. Unlike blood-soaked palace dramas, Minglan's battles are fought with tea ceremonies, marriage proposals, and carefully chosen words. The Northern Song setting is lovingly recreated, from fashion and food to the rigid social customs that govern every interaction. Zhao Liying delivers a career-defining performance, and the show richly rewards viewers who appreciate slow-burn storytelling with emotional payoffs that land perfectly.

## Empresses in the Palace (2012) — Deadly Concubine Politics
![Empresses in the Palace](https://image.tmdb.org/t/p/w342/iOMoMkDAAS2lJ1p5yCnKhrToWqc.jpg)

**Rating:** 8.5 | **Episodes:** 76 | **Genres:** historical, drama

A 17-year-old innocent enters the imperial harem and must learn to survive deadly concubine politics in the Qing court. [Empresses in the Palace](/en/drama/empresses-in-the-palace) is essential viewing for anyone drawn to the [historical](/en/best/historical) genre. Its brilliance lies in refusing to simplify anyone: every woman, no matter how ruthless, is driven by fears and desires you can almost understand. The Qing Dynasty palace setting feels lived-in, and the psychological depth is genuinely gripping. This is the show that redefined palace drama for a generation.

## The Prisoner of Beauty (2025) — Warlord-Era Romance
![The Prisoner of Beauty](https://image.tmdb.org/t/p/w342/AZdlvg8Rij2bBkV0V9vJxsejZ1.jpg)

**Rating:** 8.5 | **Episodes:** 36 | **Genres:** historical, romance, drama

A brilliant woman in a war-torn Republic-era China becomes entangled with a powerful and enigmatic warlord. [The Prisoner of Beauty](/en/drama/the-prisoner-of-beauty) brings both vulnerability and razor-sharp intelligence to its heroine. The cinematography is gorgeous, the costume design is sumptuous, and the central romance is refreshingly complex rather than formulaic. It is also one of the more recent entries proving that the [period drama](/en/best/historical) genre continues to evolve. At 36 episodes, it is tight and propulsive, making it a great starting point for newcomers.

**Wuxia and Martial Arts: Honor and Sword**

## Mysterious Lotus Casebook (2023) — Wandering Detective
![Mysterious Lotus Casebook](https://image.tmdb.org/t/p/w342/h8DteNYVPnGn6ZgCjIlQXW5KFUb.jpg)

**Rating:** 8.5 | **Episodes:** 40 | **Genres:** wuxia, mystery

A legendary martial arts master fakes his death and resurfaces as an eccentric wandering physician, solving seemingly supernatural cases across the jianghu. [Mysterious Lotus Casebook](/en/drama/mysterious-lotus-casebook) proves that great [wuxia](/en/best/wuxia) does not require a romance at its center. The case-of-the-week format keeps the momentum going, while the central trio's evolving bond provides genuine emotional depth. Cheng Yi is magnetic in the lead role, and the show earned a rare second season that many fans consider even stronger than the first. It is already featured on our [best historical list](/en/best/historical).

## My Journey to You (2023) — Fortress Infiltration
![My Journey to You](https://image.tmdb.org/t/p/w342/dh23XE6xjTTcXM7i3EU3a4N93KQ.jpg)

**Rating:** 8.5 | **Episodes:** 24 | **Genres:** wuxia, action, drama

A deadly assassin infiltrates a powerful family fortress by posing as a prospective bride, only to discover that nothing is what it seems. [My Journey to You](/en/drama/my-journey-to-you) reimagines the infiltration trope through a distinctly [wuxia](/en/best/wuxia) lens. The all-female creative team brings a fresh perspective to action choreography and character dynamics. At just 24 episodes, it is one of the tightest dramas on this list, packing stunning visuals and genuine twists into a compact runtime.

**Fantasy and Xianxia: Immortals and Demons**

## Love and Redemption (2020) — Reincarnation Romance
![Love and Redemption](https://image.tmdb.org/t/p/w342/msxa8jgmKOZFUf9gycj6PgXSUW2.jpg)

**Rating:** 8.6 | **Episodes:** 59 | **Genres:** fantasy, romance, drama

Star-crossed souls reincarnate across lifetimes, fighting fate and forbidden love in a world of cultivation and celestial hierarchy. [Love and Redemption](/en/drama/love-and-redemption) takes the [xianxia](/en/best/fantasy) romance formula to its emotional extreme. Its central conceit, the female lead who cannot feel emotions while the male lead slowly unlocks her heart across lifetimes, is devastating in the best possible way. The show demands patience through its 59-episode run, but the emotional payoff in the final arcs is extraordinary. If you want a fantasy romance that will genuinely make you weep, this is the one.

## The Untamed (2019) — Cultivation Mystery
![The Untamed](https://image.tmdb.org/t/p/w342/1KS9QjE0JlKe7Dk30yPLO42J7Kd.jpg)

**Rating:** 8.5 | **Episodes:** 50 | **Genres:** fantasy, action, drama

Two disciples of rival cultivation clans uncover a dark secret buried for sixteen years, navigating betrayal, resurrection, and the price of loyalty. [The Untamed](/en/drama/the-untamed) became a global phenomenon for good reason. At its core, it is a mystery about what really happened during a fifteen-year gap, but it is equally a profound story about sacrifice, justice, and the bonds that outlast death. The [fantasy](/en/best/fantasy) cultivation world is richly imagined with distinct clan cultures, the lead chemistry is electric, and the non-linear storytelling keeps you guessing until the final episodes. For many international viewers, this was their gateway into Chinese dramas.

## Joy of Life (2019) — Court Comedy with a Twist
![Joy of Life](https://image.tmdb.org/t/p/w342/wHJvPo9CLpXwX1ncDg6uD0QJIZo.jpg)

**Rating:** 8.6 | **Episodes:** 46 | **Genres:** historical, fantasy, comedy

A young man with mysterious modern-world knowledge navigates court intrigue with humor, wit, and unexpected martial arts talent. [Joy of Life](/en/drama/joy-of-life) is the rare historical drama that makes you laugh out loud while keeping the political stakes high. The fish-out-of-water premise generates both comedy and genuine tension, and the worldbuilding is surprisingly layered. It bridges [historical](/en/best/historical) and [fantasy](/en/best/fantasy) genres effortlessly, proving that period dramas do not have to be solemn to be great.

## What Makes a Great Historical Chinese Drama?

The best historical Chinese dramas share more than elaborate costumes and sweeping landscapes. At their core, they explore timeless human conflicts: loyalty versus ambition, love versus duty, truth versus survival. They invest in production authenticity, from the architecture and weaponry to the social hierarchies and ritual customs of their chosen era. And perhaps most importantly, they use history as a lens to examine questions that still resonate today, asking what power costs, what honor demands, and how ordinary people navigate extraordinary circumstances.

Whether you prefer the cerebral chess matches of court intrigue, the honor-bound world of wuxia, or the otherworldly beauty of xianxia fantasy, the genre has something extraordinary to offer. Start with the highest-rated entries on this list, then follow the tags that match your taste. The only real risk is that you will never want to stop watching, and honestly, we would not blame you for that.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the highest-rated historical Chinese drama?

Nirvana in Fire (2015) holds the top spot with a rating of 9.1. Its intricate political plotting, outstanding performances, and meticulous production design have made it the benchmark against which all other Chinese historical dramas are measured.

### Are Chinese period dramas based on real history?

Many are inspired by real dynasties and events, but most take significant creative liberties. Nirvana in Fire is set in a fictional dynasty, while Story of Minglan and Empresses in the Palace draw on the Northern Song and Qing dynasties respectively. The Legend of Tianxing is loosely based on real events during the late Qing period. Check each drama's page for its specific historical background.

### Which historical Chinese drama is best for beginners?

If you are new to the genre, we recommend starting with The Untamed (fantasy) or Joy of Life (comedy-historical), both of which have accessible storytelling and strong international fanbases. For pure court intrigue without fantasy elements, Nirvana in Fire is the essential entry point.

### How many episodes do historical Chinese dramas typically have?

Runtimes vary widely. The average falls between 40 and 60 episodes, though some epics like Story of Minglan run to 78 episodes. Newer productions like The Prisoner of Beauty (36 episodes) and My Journey to You (24 episodes) trend shorter. Regardless of length, the dramas on this list maintain strong pacing throughout.
